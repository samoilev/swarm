# Offline map vectors

`ne_110m_land.geojson` and `ne_110m_admin_0_boundary_lines_land.geojson` are the
1:110m Natural Earth vector datasets from the `nvkelso/natural-earth-vector` mirror.
Natural Earth data is in the public domain. The files are bundled only for the
offline `Canvas` renderer; the app does not download tiles or instantiate MapKit in
offline mode.

Source: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-cultural-vectors/
