# Third-party notices

Swarm bundles no third-party Swift packages. It does bundle third-party **data**, listed
below. The [GPL-3.0 license](LICENSE) covers Swarm's own source code — it does not
relicense these datasets.

## Bundled datasets

| File | Source | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Swarm/Core/Resources/place_index_v2.tsv` | [GeoNames daily export](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/) | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| `Swarm/Core/Resources/ne_110m_land.geojson` | [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) 1:110m | Public domain |
| `Swarm/Core/Resources/ne_110m_admin_0_boundary_lines_land.geojson` | [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) 1:110m | Public domain |

The place index is a **modified** bilingual snapshot of GeoNames, version
`geonames-2026-07-28`: every former-USSR populated place, plus places over 500 people and
capital and admin seats across Europe and North America. It is generated by
`Scripts/generate_place_index.py` and documented in
[`PLACE_DATA.md`](Swarm/Core/Resources/PLACE_DATA.md). The Natural Earth vectors are used
only by the offline map renderer, and are documented in
[`MAP_DATA.md`](Swarm/Core/Resources/MAP_DATA.md).

> Contains GeoNames data, available from https://www.geonames.org/.

## Example tree media

The six example trees in [`Examples/`](Examples/) ship 172 portraits and 12 documentary
attachments from Wikimedia Commons and comparable archives. 171 of the 184 files are
public domain and 2 are CC0; the remaining 11 are Creative Commons Attribution or
Attribution-ShareAlike. Per-file title, license, attribution and source page are in
[`Examples/CREDITS.csv`](Examples/CREDITS.csv) — redistribute the CC BY and CC BY-SA files
with that attribution intact.
